## Overriding this component

Full model: [docs/overriding.md](../../../../../docs/overriding.md).

### 1. Tokens — rebrand every label at once

Override `--ui-color-foreground` / `--ui-color-danger` on `:root` or scoped under `[data-theme]`.

### 2. `labelVariants` — style something that isn't a `Label`

```tsx
<span className={labelVariants()}>Email</span>
```

## Associating with a control

<Canvas of={LabelStories.AssociatedWithInput} />

Clicking the label focuses (and, for Checkbox/Switch/RadioGroup, activates) the control — this
works even though those controls render as `<button>`, because Radix's `Label` forwards the
click through `htmlFor` rather than relying only on native `<label>`/`<input>` pairing.

## When to reach for this vs. `FormField`

`FormField` renders its own label internally and is the right choice for a single control with a
label/hint/error. Use `Label` directly when labelling something `FormField` doesn't fit — a bare
control outside a form, or a heading-style label inside a `Fieldset`.
