## Overriding this component

Full model: [docs/overriding.md](../../../../../docs/overriding.md).

`--ui-color-border`, `--ui-color-surface`, `--ui-shadow-md` on `:root` or scoped under

Both exported publicly.

## Popover vs. Dialog vs. Tooltip

- **Popover** — non-modal, no focus trap, no backdrop. Filters, a details flyout, a mini form.
- **Dialog** — modal, traps focus, renders a backdrop. Confirmations, forms that block the page.
- **Tooltip** — no focus at all, opens on hover/focus, disappears on blur. A one-line hint.
