## Overriding this component

Full model: [docs/overriding.md](../../../../../docs/overriding.md).

### 1. Tokens — rebrand every fieldset at once

Override `--ui-color-foreground` / `--ui-color-danger` on `:root` or scoped under `[data-theme]`.

### 2. `fieldsetVariants` / `legendVariants` — style something that isn't a `Fieldset`

```tsx
<div className={fieldsetVariants({ gap: "sm" })} />
```

### 3. `classNames` — reach a specific part

```tsx
<Fieldset legend="Shipping method" classNames={{ legend: "text-lg" }} />
```

## Grouping a `RadioGroup` or a run of `Checkbox`es

<Canvas of={FieldsetStories.CheckboxGroup} />

`Fieldset` gives a *set* of controls one accessible name via `<legend>`, which is different from
`FormField`'s job of wiring a label/hint/error to a *single* control. Nest a `FormField` (or a
bare `RadioGroup`) inside a `Fieldset` when you need both: the group-level legend plus a
per-control error.
