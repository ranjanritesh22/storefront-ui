## When to reach for this instead of Dialog/Drawer

`Dialog` and `Drawer` already render their own overlay (`DialogOverlay`/`DrawerOverlay`) as part
of their Radix-backed open/close lifecycle — don't pair `Backdrop` with either. Reach for
`Backdrop` directly only for a custom surface that isn't a Radix Dialog at all, most commonly a
section-level loading overlay (see above) or a bespoke non-modal panel that still needs to dim the
page behind it.
